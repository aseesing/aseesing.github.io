My name Arjan Seesing. My oldest friends used to call me (a) zeikerd. I can be nitpicky and sometimes have strong 
feelings about tabs or spaces. 
I used to build trading systems for around 15 years. Why? Because it was fun and it paid well.
Now a bit later, I still come back to it sometimes. What can I build, not with a team of people around me, but with
a small team of AI agents. Can I convert my experience to something slightly competitive?
What I liked the most was the exchange connectivity: the part that turns orders into
bytes on the wire and the exchange's replies back into fills. Or converting the public feed messages back in the 
order book on our trading systems. These problems are mostly solved by now. But my background was mostly Java,
so I missed out on some of the cooler techniques.
Not sure what you will find here in a few months or years. But the plan is to write about trading tech: latency, 
protocols, system design, programming languages, AI and the job market, and about
what I've learned as a developer along the way. You can find me on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/seesing) and [GitHub](https://github.com/aseesing).
